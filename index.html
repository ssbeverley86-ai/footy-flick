FOOTY FLICK — FIRST KICK GUIDE

1. Touch the football and flick upward. A longer flick means more power.

2. Curve your swipe left or right to guide a snap, banana or ground dribbler. Spin is automatic.

3. For a flashing GROUND BALL challenge, use a short curved swipe. Watch the first bounce and the direction change.

4. In arcade mode, kick THROUGH gaps, HIT targets, or LAND in containers. The target tells you what to do.

5. Test wind by tossing grass; in rain, wipe the ball quickly; shade a low sun by holding it.

6. Score a goal with enough carry for the teammate behind the posts to mark it and earn +3 bonus points.

7. For arcade shots, the green power band is the sweet spot. Different targets require passing through, hitting, or landing in something.
