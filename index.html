Credits, privacy and content guidelines

Footy Flick is an independent, fictional Australian rules kicking game. Not affiliated with any football league, club or professional player. All stadium branding, players, names and game artwork in this preview are fictional.

Only upload team images you own or have permission to use. Uploaded emblems and local scores are stored in this browser; they are not publicly shared by this prototype. No accounts or in-game analytics are included. The graphics library is fetched from the jsDelivr content delivery network, so that network receives a technical request.
